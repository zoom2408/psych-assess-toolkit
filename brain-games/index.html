🕹️ Brain Games

A quick, fun break between assessments. Four free games, no sign-up: a live trivia quiz (pulled from a public trivia database), a reaction time test, sudoku, and 2048. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is saved except your local best scores.

Just for fun — trivia questions are supplied live by the free, public Open Trivia Database API. Reaction time, sudoku, and 2048 run entirely on your device.
