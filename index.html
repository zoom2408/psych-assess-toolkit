About this hub

Each assessment on this page is a standalone, self-contained tool built for reflection and personal insight. They combine well-known psychological frameworks into a single guided experience with a results summary you can review or export. None of these tools are clinical or diagnostic instruments — they're meant for self-discovery and educational purposes only. If you're struggling with stress, mental health, or wellbeing, please reach out to a qualified healthcare professional.
